Innovation in Outpatient Care

Providing quality healthcare and patient satisfaction through digital transformation.

Our Purpose

The Outpatient Department (OPD) serves as the primary entry point to hospital care, providing essential diagnostic and treatment services. To address the challenges of congestion and prolonged waiting lines, we have implemented telehealth services. This initiative ensures that healthcare is more accessible, secure, and cost-effective for all our patients.

Our Mission

To provide continuity of care through remote consultations, making healthcare services easily accessible even to patients in remote areas.

Our Vision

To reduce overcrowding in hospital facilities while improving patient satisfaction and supporting health education through digital innovation.

Core Objectives

Our telehealth platform is designed with the following goals in mind:

  • Reduce overcrowding and wait times
  • Improve access to specialist care
  • Provide timely and remote consultations
  • Ensure safety and infection control
  • Maintain high data confidentiality
  • Optimize the healthcare workforce

Continuity of Care

By integrating Electronic Medical Records (EMR) with advanced nursing informatics, we ensure that every virtual visit is documented with accuracy and care. We remain dedicated to monitoring your recovery and health compliance through proactive follow-up evaluations and remote support.
