Our Telehealth Services

Accessible, safe, and cost-effective healthcare for Outpatient Department (OPD) patients, ensuring continuity of care from the safety of your home.

Consultations & Follow-ups

Schedule remote appointments for initial consultations and regular follow-up checkups with our specialists[cite: 107].

Results Review

Seamless review of laboratory and diagnostic results with your healthcare provider through our secure platform[cite: 108].

E-Prescriptions

Receive digital medication prescriptions sent directly to our pharmacy for faster processing and remote counseling[cite: 110, 153].

Remote Monitoring

Ongoing monitoring of symptoms and vital signs checklists to ensure your path to recovery is on track[cite: 111, 125].

How Telehealth Works

1

Patient Registration

Register on our portal with your basic information and preferred consultation type (Zoom, Messenger, or Face-to-Face) [cite: 116-118].

2

Nurse Triage

A nurse reviews your symptoms and history to determine if your case is suitable for a virtual or in-person visit [cite: 120-123].

3

Virtual Consultation

Connect with your doctor via secure audio-video for a clinical evaluation and personalized care plan [cite: 133-137].

4

Clinical Decision

Your doctor provides a diagnosis, treatment plan, and real-time documentation in your Electronic Medical Record (EMR) [cite: 140-145].
